About Bubble Pop

Bubble Pop is a free, browser-based bubble shooter puzzle game featuring 45 levels of colorful, addictive gameplay. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to clear the board and progress through increasingly challenging levels.

How to Play

Aim your bubble using touch or mouse, then tap to shoot. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. Clear all bubbles before running out of moves to complete each level and earn stars.

Features

45 unique levels with increasing difficulty, star ratings based on performance, and progress saved automatically on your device.
